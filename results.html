Results

The final results of the FIRE 2026 Shared Task on AI4Industrial Automation are presented separately for the two subtasks. The rankings are based on the final evaluation score obtained on the official test data.

Evaluation Formula

The final score is calculated by comparing the generated program with the corresponding Gold Standard Code.

Final Score = 0.25 × Cosine Similarity + 0.25 × Structural Match Score + 0.50 × PDG Score
Metric Weight
Cosine Similarity (CS) 25%
Structural Match Score (SMS) 25%
Program Dependence Graph Similarity (PDG) 50%
Note: The final ranking is determined separately for each subtask. A separate leaderboard is provided for Task A (Natural Language to Structured Text) and Task B (Natural Language to Rust Program).
